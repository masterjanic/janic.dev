## 2. Hosting and delivery

This website is hosted and delivered through the global network of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

Each time you open a page, your browser sends technically necessary data to Cloudflare:

- your IP address
- the date and time of the request
- the requested address, the status code, and the amount of data transferred
- the referring page, if your browser sends it
- your browser identifier (user agent)

**Purpose and legal basis.** This data is needed to deliver the website, keep it stable, and protect it from attacks and abuse. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of this website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Cloudflare's role.** Cloudflare acts as my processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). For some of the data it collects when running and securing its network, Cloudflare acts as an independent controller.

**Storage.** I do not store or read individual access logs for this website. In the Cloudflare dashboard I can see aggregated statistics, such as request numbers. How long Cloudflare keeps data is described in the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**Transfers outside the EU.** Cloudflare may process data in the USA and in other countries. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). If that certification no longer applies, Cloudflare relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

## 3. Cookies and similar technologies

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser's local storage.

If a request looks suspicious, Cloudflare's security systems may run a browser check and set a strictly necessary security cookie, such as `cf_clearance` or `__cf_bm`. The legal basis for storing and reading it is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, and for the related processing Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Details are in Cloudflare's [cookie documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/policies-compliances/cloudflare-cookies/).

The copy button on code blocks runs only in your browser and sends nothing to anyone.

## 5. Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and virtbase.com. None of them are embedded, so no data reaches them unless you follow a link. If you do, your browser connects to that website and normally tells it that you came from janic.dev. From then on, that website's own privacy policy applies.

## 6. RSS feed
